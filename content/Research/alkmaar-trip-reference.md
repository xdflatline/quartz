## 2. Cheese Market (Waagplein, Alkmaar)

The full traditional Kaasmarkt with cheese carriers and the weigh-house ceremony runs on Friday mornings only during the in-season window (late March through late September). The market operates from 10:00 with the carrier procession and weighing demo running roughly 10:00-12:30.

In-stay Friday dates: **July 17, 2026** and **July 24, 2026**.

A smaller-scale "cheese experience" / tourist-focused variant operates on some Wednesday evenings and Saturdays in July. The main carriers-and-bells ceremony is the Friday morning event.

Directions from the Airbnb to Waagplein:
https://www.google.com/maps/dir/?api=1&origin=52.685802,4.702429&destination=52.6008538,4.8170994

Reference: Waagplein, Alkmaar center; look for the medieval weigh house (De Waag). Free to spectate; stands sit in a ring around the square.

## 4. Trip-Planning Notes

- The Airbnb location is in the dune belt, not in Alkmaar city. Expect a 15-20 minute drive or a bus ride into the center for the cheese market or a city supermarket run.
- For the cheese market on Friday July 17, plan to be at Waagplein by 10:00 for the opening ceremony; carriers typically run their first weighing slot around 10:00-10:30 and a second around 11:00-11:30.
- For groceries on a Sunday, Albert Heijn Bergen (1.6 km) is the safest single stop with the longest Sunday hours. DekaMarkt Bergen (1.8 km) is the second-best. Avoid relying on Lidl in Alkmaar on Sundays.
- Public transport: the 9292 planner is the authoritative Dutch trip planner. The maps skill can also pull routes from OpenStreetMap/OSRM as a fallback when 9292 is unreachable.

## 6. Dunes, Beach, and Walking Trips

The Schoorlse Duinen is a large contiguous dune and forest reserve west of the stay. Reverse geocoding puts the canonical main entrance on Schoorlse Zeeweg, Schoorl. Distances below are road-network distance from the Airbnb; OSRM does not return realistic walking speeds for Dutch coastal paths, so realistic times assume 5 km/h walking and 15 km/h cycling.

### 6.1 Schoorlse Duinen (main entrance, west)

- Coordinates: 52.7008, 4.6640
- Distance: 4.6 km road
- Time: ~30 min walking, ~18 min cycling
- Use: 1,800 ha of dunes, forest, and the Klimmen duin (highest in the Netherlands). Multiple colour-coded walking routes from 1.5 km to 18 km, well-marked. The entrance at Schoorlse Zeeweg is the most car/bike-friendly start; an alternative north entrance sits near the Camperduin beach.
- Map link: https://www.google.com/maps/dir/?api=1&origin=52.685802,4.702429&destination=52.6978888,4.6634659

### 6.2 Bergen aan Zee (south coast)

- Coordinates: 52.6608, 4.6317
- Distance: 7.8 km road
- Time: ~30 min cycling, ~95 min walking
- Use: Sandy beach, lighthouse, beach pavilions (Noorderlicht, Lekkers aan Zee) for food. Quieter than Camperduin. Combine with the southern Schoorlse Duinen for a half-day ride.
- Map link: https://www.google.com/maps/dir/?api=1&origin=52.685802,4.702429&destination=52.6608,4.6317

### 6.3 Camperduin (north coast)

- Coordinates: 52.7255, 4.6515
- Distance: 7.0 km road
- Time: ~28 min cycling, ~85 min walking
- Use: Wider beach, popular with families. Bounded by the Hondsbossche Zeewering sea defence and the northern tip of the Schoorlse Duinen. Pairs well with a return leg through the dune interior.
- Map link: https://www.google.com/maps/dir/?api=1&origin=52.685802,4.702429&destination=52.7255,4.6515

### 6.4 Bergen village (south, inland)

- Coordinates: ~52.6705, 4.7032 (Plein / Breelaan)
- Distance: 1.7 km road
- Time: ~20 min walking, ~7 min cycling
- Use: Bergen artist village with galleries, the Ruinekerk, and the historic centre. Best for a short evening walk or a coffee stop. The supermarket cluster (AH, ALDI, DekaMarkt) is also here.

### 6.5 Suggested day routes

- Short loop (~1.5-2 hours): walk to the main Schoorlse Duinen entrance, take the shortest colour-coded loop (~3 km inside the dunes), return via Duinweg.
- Half-day bike loop (~3-4 hours): bike south to Bergen aan Zee along the coastal road, lunch at a beach pavilion, return via the dune interior and Bergen village.
- Full-day bike loop (~5-6 hours): north to Camperduin, return via the northern Schoorlse Duinen trail, lunch in Schoorl, then south through Bergen village.

## 7. Trip-Planning Notes (continued)

- The Schoorlse Duinen walking routes are colour-coded on yellow / red / purple / green signposts at every trail junction. Pick by posted walking time, not by loop length, since the dune terrain adds time.
- Bikes must stay on designated paths inside the Schoorlse Duinen. Walking-only dune zones are marked; do not shortcut through them.
- For sunset, Bergen aan Zee faces west and gives the cleanest view back over the dunes; Camperduin faces north and is better for late afternoon.
- For a small-children-friendly bike ride, stick to the Duinweg / Heereweg corridor between Schoorl and Bergen; the dune interior paths are loose sand and unsuitable for kids on standard bikes.
