# Haarlem Day Trip Reference

**Updated:** 2026-07-13
**Anchor:** Haarlem station, Stationsplein 1, 2011 LR Haarlem (52.3880778, 4.6385788)
**Day-trip origin:** Bergen / Schoorl stay (Boerderij Hazendaal), via Alkmaar station
**Train connection:** Alkmaar → Haarlem direct, ~41-49 min (NS Sprinter / Intercity, ~2 per hour, ~41 trains/day)

## 1. Getting There

Haarlem is the natural day trip from the Bergen / Schoorl area: a direct train from Alkmaar (the nearest mainline station to the stay) takes 41-49 minutes end-to-end, with roughly two departures per hour and no transfers. Plan ~1 h from the stay to Haarlem station door-to-door including the bus or drive from Aagtdorp to Alkmaar.

NS Journey Planner is the authoritative source for live timetables and any 2026 diversions. The maps skill's OSRM distance is ~55 km by road from the stay to Haarlem station; the train routing is shorter.

| Leg | Mode | Time | Frequency |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Stay → Alkmaar station | bus / car | ~15-20 min | varies |
| Alkmaar → Haarlem | NS Sprinter / Intercity direct | 41-49 min | ~2/hour, ~41/day |
| Haarlem station → Grote Markt (city center) | walk | ~15-20 min on foot | n/a |

Haarlem station sits ~1.0 km north of the Grote Markt, a flat 15-20 minute walk south through Stationsplein and the pedestrian Kruisweg / Barteljorisstraat corridor. Buses 2, 3, 4, 12, 14, 73, 75 and others stop at the station if you prefer to ride; nothing in the itinerary below needs anything other than walking from the station.

## 2. Things to See (foot-reachable from Haarlem station)

All entries below are within ~1.9 km of Haarlem station and are reached on foot via the Grote Markt / Spaarne corridor. Realistic walking-time estimates assume ~5 km/h on foot and use `~` to flag them as approximate; OSRM road distances are stated exactly as reported.

### 2.1 Grote Markt (city center square)

- Coordinates: 52.3813667, 4.6360934
- Distance from station: 1.9 km road (OSRM)
- Walking time: ~20-25 min
- What it is: The central market square of Haarlem since the Middle Ages, ringed by the Grote Kerk, the Vleeshal, the Verweyhal, the town hall, and ~30 café terraces. Hosts a general market on Saturdays (~08:30-17:00, per visithaarlem.com) and a clothing-focused market on Mondays (per Rick Steves and Tripadvisor). The square itself is open 24/7; terraces and market stalls have their own hours.

### 2.2 Grote Kerk (Sint-Bavokerk)

- Coordinates: 52.3810332, 4.6381159 (Grote Markt 22, 2011 RD Haarlem)
- Distance from station: 1.9 km road (OSRM)
- Walking time: ~20-25 min
- What it is: Late-Gothic cruciform church built ~1370-1520, known for its wooden vault (often compared to an upturned hull), the Christian Müller organ that Mozart played as a child, and a crypt containing historic grave slabs. The tower (Bavotoren) is a separate entry and is not always open to climb. Open to visitors outside service hours; entry is paid (per bavo.nl, the church's own site).

### 2.3 Frans Hals Museum

- Coordinates: 52.3766062, 4.6336897 (Groot Heiligland 62, 2011 ES Haarlem)
- Distance from station: 2.7 km road (OSRM)
- Walking time: ~30-35 min
- What it is: Two-location museum of Dutch and Haarlem Golden Age painting anchored by the world's largest collection of Frans Hals group portraits, plus pre-modern and modern/contemporary wings. The main site (Groot Heiligland 62) sits 550 m south of the Grote Markt; the "Hal" site is on the Grote Markt itself.

### 2.4 Teylers Museum

- Coordinates: 52.3803526, 4.6403433 (Spaarne 16, 2011 CH Haarlem)
- Distance from station: 1.8 km road (OSRM)
- Walking time: ~20-25 min
- What it is: The oldest public museum in the Netherlands, in continuous operation since 1778 (open to the public since 1784), housed in an 18th-century neoclassical building on the Spaarne river. Permanent collections cover art of the Old Masters (Rembrandt, Michelangelo drawings), 18th-19th-century science instruments, minerals and fossils, and a fossil room. Permanent collection is free for kids under 5; paid for adults.

### 2.5 Corrie ten Boom Museum

- Coordinates: 52.3822632, 4.635411 (Barteljorisstraat 19, 2011 RA Haarlem)
- Distance from station: 1.4 km road (OSRM)
- Walking time: ~15-20 min
- What it is: Small house museum in the former clockmaker's shop where the ten Boom family hid Jews and others during the Nazi occupation. Corrie ten Boom and her family were arrested in 1944; Corrie survived and became a writer and speaker. The museum is run by the ten Boom family and emphasizes the family's Christian faith and the broader resistance network. The house is narrow, with a steep staircase and no wheelchair access.

### 2.6 Molen De Adriaan

- Coordinates: 52.3837911, 4.6426522 (Papentorenvest 1A, 2011 AV Haarlem)
- Distance from station: 1.2 km road (OSRM)
- Walking time: ~12-15 min
- What it is: A smock windmill (stellingmolen type) on the bank of the river Spaarne. The original 1779 mill burned in 1932, was rebuilt in 2002, and operates today as a working museum. Views from the upper stages cover central Haarlem. Visitor access is by guided tour only (per the mill's own site, molenadriaan.nl).

### 2.7 Jopenkerk

- Coordinates: 52.3811942, 4.629891 (Gedempte Voldersgracht 2, 2011 WD Haarlem; building also fronts Hortusplein)
- Distance from station: 3.0 km road (OSRM)
- Walking time: ~35-40 min
- What it is: Beer brewery, tasting room, and restaurant inside a former Jacobskerk church, founded by the Jopen brewery to revive historical Haarlem beer recipes. Long shared tables, a copper brewhouse in the nave, and a long beer menu. Open daily for lunch and dinner from late morning; kitchen hours are on the brewery's own site.

### 2.8 Haarlemmerhout (park)

- Coordinates: 52.3677325, 4.6228308 (park polygon centroid; main entrance on Zandvoorterpad, 2012 JK Haarlem)
- Distance from station: 3.3 km road (OSRM)
- Walking time: ~40-45 min
- What it is: Oldest public park in the Netherlands, a 55 ha woodland just south of the old city walls. Lawn, mature beech and oak, walking paths, a pavilion with a café. Combines with the walk from Grote Markt south for a 2-3 hour afternoon loop.

## 3. Suggested Day Route

A flat, foot-only loop that hits the major sights in ~5-6 hours of walking plus museum/cafe stops. Best done on a Saturday to combine with the Grote Markt market.

1. **09:30** Haarlem station → walk south along Stationsplein / Kruisweg to Grote Markt (~20-25 min). Stop at the Saturday market stalls.
2. **10:30** Grote Kerk (Sint-Bavokerk) on the west side of the Grote Markt. ~45 min inside for the church + crypt.
3. **11:30** Walk 3 min west to Teylers Museum on the Spaarne. ~1.5 h for the permanent collection.
4. **13:00** Lunch on the Grote Markt terraces, or detour west to Jopenkerk for brewery lunch (~5 min from Teylers).
5. **14:30** Walk south 10 min to Frans Hals Museum. ~1.5 h for the Golden Age collection.
6. **16:15** Walk east 10 min to Corrie ten Boom Museum for the scheduled guided tour (~45 min).
7. **17:00** Walk 10 min west to Molen De Adriaan for the late-afternoon mill tour (~45 min).
8. **18:00** Walk back through the Grote Markt to Haarlem station (~20-25 min). Total: ~8 h including all stops.

On a Monday, skip Frans Hals and Teylers (both closed) and put more time into the Monday clothing market, the Grote Kerk, and De Adriaan. The Corrie ten Boom Museum is open on Mondays but by guided tour only.

## 4. Trip-Planning Notes

- The entire day-trip core is within 1.9 km of Haarlem station; nothing in §2 requires a car or any public transport beyond the train in and out.
- The Saturday general market on the Grote Markt is the strongest reason to schedule this trip for a Saturday. Monday has a smaller clothing market but loses two of the three major museums.
- For 2026, Teylers Museum is open Mondays 10:00-17:00 until 24 August 2026 (per teylersmuseum.nl) - a useful exception to the standard Monday closure.
- Frans Hals Museum and Corrie ten Boom Museum both recommend or require online booking, especially in summer. Walk-up tickets are possible but not guaranteed.
- Jopenkerk is a real brewery - the brewhouse runs in the nave - so expect some ambient noise and a more pub-like atmosphere than a typical restaurant.
- Haarlemmerhout is the right end-of-day option if you have time and want a quieter walk; skip it if you want to catch an early train back.
- The Schoorlse Duinen tourist office and the Bergen village VVV can both print the current day's 9292 itinerary; the NS app is the more reliable day-of planner.
