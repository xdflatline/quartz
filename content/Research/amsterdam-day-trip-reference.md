# Amsterdam Day Trip Reference

**Updated:** 2026-07-13
**Anchor:** Amsterdam Centraal station, Stationsplein, 1012 AB Amsterdam (52.378901, 4.9005805)
**Day-trip origin:** Bergen / Schoorl stay (Boerderij Hazendaal), via Alkmaar station
**Train connection:** Alkmaar → Amsterdam Centraal direct, ~35-38 min (NS Intercity, 4 per hour)

## 1. Getting There

Amsterdam is the second of the two natural day trips from the Bergen / Schoorl area. The direct Intercity from Alkmaar to Amsterdam Centraal runs every 15 minutes, takes 35-38 minutes, and does not require a transfer. Plan ~1 h 15 min door-to-door from the stay to the Centraal station hall, including the bus or drive from Aagtdorp to Alkmaar.

| Leg | Mode | Time | Frequency |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Stay → Alkmaar station | bus / car | ~15-20 min | varies |
| Alkmaar → Amsterdam Centraal | NS Intercity direct | 35-38 min | 4/hour |
| Amsterdam Centraal → Anne Frank House | walk | ~30-35 min | n/a |
| Amsterdam Centraal → Rijksmuseum | tram 2 or 12 | ~14 min ride + 3 min walk | 5-10 min |
| Amsterdam Centraal → Albert Cuyp market | metro 52 (De Pijp stop) | ~5 min ride | every 10 min |

Amsterdam Centraal sits on the north edge of the historic center. Most day-trip POIs in this list are south of the station, in the Grachtengordel (canal belt) and the Museumkwartier (south of the center). The walking-only route to Anne Frank House and Westerkerk is flat and follows the canal grid; the museum quarter is far enough south that a tram is the standard move.

### Transit from Centraal

GVB (Amsterdam transit operator) sells single-ride and day tickets. A standard 1-hour GVB ticket is sufficient for the tram/metro hops in this itinerary; a 1- to 7-day GVB day ticket is cheaper if you expect 3+ hops.

| Line | From Centraal to | Where to board | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Tram 2 | Museumplein (Rijksmuseum, Van Gogh) | West side of Centraal, tram stop "Centraal Station" | ~14 min ride, every 5-10 min |
| Tram 12 | Museumplein (alt route) | West side of Centraal | same Museumplein terminus, slightly different mid-route |
| Tram 13 / 14 / 17 | Elandsgracht / Marnixstraat / West | West side of Centraal | useful for the Jordaan side |
| Metro 52 (Noord/Zuidlijn) | De Pijp (Albert Cuyp market), Vijzelgracht, Rokin | Underneath Centraal, "Centraal Station" metro hall | 4 min to De Pijp, every 10 min |
| Metro 51 / 53 / 54 | Amstel, Bijlmer, Isolatorweg | Underneath Centraal | not needed for this itinerary |

## 2. Things to See (foot or transit-reachable from Centraal)

All entries below are reachable on foot or by a single tram/metro hop from Amsterdam Centraal. Realistic walking-time estimates assume ~5 km/h on foot and use `~` to flag them as approximate; OSRM road distances are stated exactly as reported.

### 2.1 Anne Frank House (Anne Frank Huis)

- Coordinates: 52.3751558, 4.8840807 (Prinsengracht 267, 1016 GV Amsterdam)
- Distance from Centraal: 2.8 km road (OSRM)
- Walking time: ~30-35 min
- What it is: The canal house and hidden annex where Anne Frank, her family, and four others hid from the Nazi occupation from 1942 to 1944. Preserved as a museum with Anne's original diary, the bookcase door, and the empty annex rooms. The visit takes ~1 hour inside.

Tickets are online-only, ~€16.50 adult / ~€7.00 child (10-17), released every Tuesday at 10:00 CEST for slots six weeks later. After 15:30 same-day tickets may be available at the door; expect a queue. Museumkaart not valid. The walk from Centraal goes through the Jordaan and across the Prinsengracht.

### 2.2 Westerkerk

- Coordinates: 52.3745473, 4.8839863 (Prinsengracht, 1015 EB Amsterdam)
- Distance from Centraal: 2.5 km road (OSRM)
- Walking time: ~25-30 min
- What it is: 17th-century Reformed Protestant church on the Prinsengracht, with the tallest church tower in Amsterdam (~85 m, "Westertoren"). Rembrandt is buried somewhere in the church, exact location unknown. Anne Frank described the Westerkerk clock from her annex and the tower is visible from the Anne Frank House courtyard.

### 2.3 Begijnhof

- Coordinates: 52.3693959, 4.889956 (Gedempte Begijnensloot, 1012 PP Amsterdam)
- Distance from Centraal: 2.9 km road (OSRM)
- Walking time: ~30-35 min
- What it is: A 14th-century enclosed courtyard that was home to the Beguines, a lay Catholic women's religious community. Today a mixed residential courtyard run by two church foundations; open to the public during daytime. The English Reformed Church sits at the east end, and the Catholic chapel (Het Houten Huys) at the west is one of the oldest surviving timber-frame houses in Amsterdam. Quiet green lawn, gravel paths, and a stone-vaulted entrance.

### 2.4 Amsterdam Museum (former civic museum)

- Coordinates: 52.3704312, 4.8904288 (Kalverstraat 92, 1012 PH Amsterdam)
- Distance from Centraal: 2.6 km road (OSRM)
- Walking time: ~25-30 min
- What it is: City museum of Amsterdam covering medieval origins through the Golden Age, the VOC era, and modern history. Housed in the former city orphanage (Burgerweeshuis), with a 17th-century courtyard and a sequence of period rooms. The Amsterdam DNA multimedia show covers the first 1000 years of the city in ~50 minutes and is a useful orientation if you have never been to Amsterdam.

### 2.5 Rijksmuseum

- Coordinates: 52.3598431, 4.8850395 (Museumstraat 1, 1071 ZC Amsterdam)
- Distance from Centraal: 5.5 km road (OSRM)
- Walking time: ~50-60 min; tram 2 or 12 from Centraal: ~14 min ride + 3 min walk
- What it is: The national museum of the Netherlands, opened in 1800, with the largest collection of Dutch Golden Age painting in the world - including Rembrandt's *The Night Watch* and Vermeer's *The Milkmaid*. The Gallery of Honour runs the spine of the building and is the main walking route. Plan 2-3 hours inside.

### 2.6 Van Gogh Museum

- Coordinates: 52.3583673, 4.88109 (Paulus Potterstraat 7, 1071 CX Amsterdam)
- Distance from Centraal: 4.9 km road (OSRM)
- Walking time: ~45-55 min; tram 2 or 12 from Centraal: ~12 min ride + 2 min walk
- What it is: The world's largest collection of Van Gogh paintings and drawings, with ~200 paintings and ~500 drawings covering his full career plus a strong collection of 19th-century French painters who influenced him. Plan 2-3 hours inside.

### 2.7 Heineken Experience

- Coordinates: 52.3578404, 4.891784 (Stadhouderskade 78, 1072 AE Amsterdam)
- Distance from Centraal: 4.5 km road (OSRM)
- Walking time: ~40-50 min; tram 4 from Centraal: ~15 min ride, or ~10 min walk from Vijzelgracht metro stop
- What it is: The former Heineken brewery (closed 1988), converted into a self-paced visitor experience about the Heineken brand and brewing process. Includes a 1st-floor ride, the bottling hall, beer tasting, and a rooftop bar with a view over the city. Plan 1.5-2 hours.

### 2.8 Vondelpark

- Coordinates: 52.3571974, 4.864119 (park polygon centroid; main entrances on Vondelstraat and Stadhouderskade)
- Distance from Centraal: 5.7 km road (OSRM)
- Walking time: ~55-65 min; tram 1 from Centraal: ~20 min ride to Vondelpark main entrance
- What it is: 47 ha English-style city park designed by the landscape architect J.D. Zocher, opened 1865, named for the playwright Joost van den Vondel. Ponds, lawns, mature trees, an open-air theatre in summer, and the Vondelkerk at the south edge. Open 24/7, free.

### 2.9 Albert Cuyp market (Albert Cuyp-markt)

- Coordinates: 52.3552329, 4.8917785 (Albert Cuypstraat, 1073 BD Amsterdam)
- Distance from Centraal: 5.5 km road (OSRM)
- Walking time: ~50-60 min; metro 52 from Centraal: 4 min to De Pijp, then surface exit to market
- What it is: The largest street market in the Netherlands, ~260 stalls running ~600 m along Albert Cuypstraat, operating Mon-Sat 09:00-17:00 (per albertcuyp-markt.amsterdam). Cheese, fish, flowers, clothing, hardware, prepared food, stroopwafels, herring, poffertjes. Closes earlier on Sundays and is closed on a small number of Dutch public holidays.

## 3. Suggested Day Route

A single-day loop hitting the historic center and Museumkwartier in ~8-9 hours including museum time. Assumes you have a 6-week-ahead timed ticket for the Anne Frank House and a separate one for the Van Gogh Museum.

1. **08:30** Alkmaar → Amsterdam Centraal (Intercity direct, 35-38 min).
2. **09:30** Walk south from Centraal to Anne Frank House on the Prinsengracht (~30-35 min). Visit the Anne Frank House on a morning slot.
3. **11:00** Walk 5 min north to Westerkerk; visit the church (~20 min). Westerkerk tower climb only if a slot is open.
4. **11:45** Walk 15 min east to the Begijnhof (~10-15 min visit).
5. **12:15** Walk 4 min east to the Amsterdam Museum. Lunch in the museum cafe or in the Spui square area.
6. **13:30** Walk back to Centraal (~20 min) and take tram 2 or 12 south to Museumplein (~14 min).
7. **14:00** Rijksmuseum (2-3 h).
8. **17:00** Walk 5 min to the Van Gogh Museum on a timed slot (1.5-2 h).
9. **19:00** Dinner in De Pijp, ~10 min walk south of the Van Gogh Museum. Albert Cuyp market runs Mon-Sat 09:00-17:00; finish before the market closes or stop by for evening drinks in one of the surrounding bars.
10. **21:00** Metro 52 north from De Pijp back to Centraal, then Intercity back to Alkmaar.

If you only have time for one of the three big museums: the Rijksmuseum is the most Amsterdam-specific, the Van Gogh is the most popular and most booked, the Anne Frank House is the most demanded timed entry.

## 4. Trip-Planning Notes

- The Anne Frank House and Van Gogh Museum both sell out days in advance during the high season (April-September). Book both at 10:00 CEST the Tuesday six weeks before your planned day; Anne Frank tickets appear at the same time six weeks out, Van Gogh tickets are continuously available but weekend slots go fast.
- The Rijksmuseum accepts walk-up tickets, but the dedicated-ticket holders line is significantly faster in the morning.
- Amsterdam Centraal is a major rail hub and a fairly long building with two tram-stop clusters. Pick the correct side: trams 2 / 12 / 13 / 14 / 17 depart from the west side ("Centraal Station" tram stop, on the Stationsplein facing the city). The metro 52 entrance is in the underground passage that runs the length of the station.
- If you are short on time, the most efficient day route is Anne Frank House → tram to Rijksmuseum → Van Gogh Museum → dinner in De Pijp. The Begijnhof and Amsterdam Museum are optional, but they pair well with the morning Anne Frank visit on foot.
- For a quieter Museumkwartier visit, go on a weekday morning. Weekends are crowded year-round; July-August queues for *The Night Watch* and the Vermeer room can be 30+ minutes.
- The Schoorlse Duinen tourist office and the Bergen village VVV can both print a 9292 itinerary; the NS app + GVB app cover the same ground with live delays.
