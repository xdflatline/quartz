## Overview

Thoughtworks is a global technology consultancy whose Insights blog publishes practitioner-facing pieces on legacy modernization, data strategy, and AI in the enterprise. The July 2026 post on ontology + LLM data modernization is authored by Zichuan Xiong, a data strategist, and reflects Thoughtworks' framing of "context as a service" at the data landscape level.
