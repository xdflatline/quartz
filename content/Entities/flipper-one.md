## Overview

[Flipper One](https://blog.flipper.net/flipper-one-we-need-your-help/) is an upcoming modular, Linux-based multi-tool designed for hardware debugging, cybersecurity research, and penetration testing. In May 2026, Flipper Devices announced an appeal to the developer community for financial, logistics, and software development assistance to bring the project to fruition.

## Technical Scope

- Powered by a quad-core ARM processor running an embedded Linux distribution.
- Modular expansion board architecture.
- Full software-defined radio (SDR) and hardware hacking peripherals.

## Development Challenges

- Company faced manufacturing bottlenecks and software integration issues, prompting a call for community contributors and hardware testers.
