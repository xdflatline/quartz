# Terminus-KIRA

**Role:** Strong human-designed coding-agent harness
**Used in:** [[Entities/meta-harness-paper]] experiments on TerminalBench-2

## Overview

The other strong starting harness used in Meta-Harness experiments on TerminalBench-2 (alongside Terminus-2). It serves the same role: a non-trivial human-designed baseline that the harness-evolution loop can improve from.

## Why Two Starting Harnesses

The Meta-Harness paper uses both Terminus-2 and Terminus-KIRA as starting points to demonstrate that the improvement is **not** a quirk of one particular initial design. Improvements from two distinct strong starting harnesses is stronger evidence that the harness design space has room above human design.
