# Díaz / Pérez research group — UPM

The Universidad Politécnica de Madrid (UPM) research group whose conceptual lineage underwrites the SDD paper.

## Authors of the SDD paper

- Jessica Díaz — yesica.diaz@upm.es
- Joaquín Gayoso — joaquin.gayoso@upm.es
- Andrea Cimminio — andrea.cimminio@upm.es
- Jorge Pérez — jorge.perez@upm.es

All affiliated with Universidad Politécnica de Madrid, Madrid, Spain.

## Lineage of relevant prior work

- **DevOps team structures: characterization and implications** (López-Fernández, Díaz, Garcia-Martin, Pérez, Gonzalez-Prieto, IEEE TSE 2021) — the paper the SDD N-to-N topology argument extends. The argument is explicitly framed as "an evolution of our previous work on DevOps Team Structures."
- **Why are many businesses instilling a DevOps culture into their organization?** (Díaz, López-Fernández, Pérez, González-Prieto, EMSE 2021) — empirical evidence about DevOps adoption that grounds the SDD adoption-as-staged-process argument.
- **Self-service cybersecurity monitoring as enabler for DevSecOps** (Díaz, Pérez, Lopez-Peña, Mena, Yagüe, IEEE Access 2019) — adjacent work in the same group.
- **Applying inter-rater reliability and agreement in collaborative grounded theory studies in software engineering** (Díaz, Pérez, Gallardo, González-Prieto, JSS 2023) — methodology the paper draws on for its own gray-literature synthesis.
- **Continuous delivery of customized SaaS edge applications in highly distributed IoT systems** (López-Viana, Díaz, Díaz, Martínez, IEEE IoT Journal 2020) — applied DevOps work.
