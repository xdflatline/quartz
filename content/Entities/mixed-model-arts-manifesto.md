# The Mixed Model Arts Manifesto (Reis, 2026)

**Category:** Article (manifesto / foundational document)
**Original URL:** https://practicaldatamodeling.substack.com/p/the-mixed-model-arts-manifesto

---

## Overview

The Mixed Model Arts Manifesto is Joe Reis's foundational statement (Aug 19, 2026) for the MMA discipline. It is published on his Substack *Practical Data Modeling* and serves as the opening text of his forthcoming book. The manifesto uses mixed martial arts as a deliberate metaphor: just as MMA in the 1990s settled centuries of "my style beats your style" arguments by unifying all styles around fundamentals, data modeling is positioned as having its own MMA moment — ending the Kimball/Inmon, normalized/denormalized, and application/analytical tribalisms.

## Key Details

- **Author:** Joe Reis
- **Published:** 2026-08-19

- **Length:** ~1,500 words of manifesto body; longer with comments
- **Structure:** Opening call → diagnosis ("house divided") → 9 numbered core beliefs → closing mission statement

### The nine core beliefs

1. Modeling is One Discipline
2. Model the Territory, Not the Technology
3. Intent Drives Structure (Operational Intent + Analytical Intent)
4. Time is Non-Negotiable
5. Semantics are the Universal Interface
6. Simplicity is a Discipline
7. Designed for Humans, Engineered for Agents
8. Modeling is a Lifecycle
9. Avoid Dogma. Be Pragmatic.

### Notable commentary in the comments

- **Larry Burns** (Aug 25, 2026): argues the real shift is that semantic data must now live *outside* both the schema and the application code so it can be consumed mechanically by AI — anticipating ontology-document generation as a first-class modeling artifact.
- **Guy Kerem** (Aug 24, 2026): asks Reis whether "demand leads to new popular interoperable ontologies emerging" — flagging the open question of whether the Agentic Era will produce de facto standard ontologies.
