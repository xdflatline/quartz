## Overview

Nielsen Norman Group (NN/g) is a user-experience research and consulting firm, co-founded by Jakob Nielsen, Don Norman, and Bruce "Tog" Tognazzini. The firm publishes evidence-based UX guidance and runs recurring surveys of UX practice across industries. In the discovery-phase context, NN/g provides the design-side definition of a discovery phase and survey data on how discovery teams are staffed in practice.

## Key Details

### Discovery-phase definition (UX side)

NN/g defines a discovery phase from the design side as "researching the problem space, framing the problems to be solved, and gathering enough evidence to decide what to do next" — complementing the public-sector framing from the GOV.UK Service Manual.

### Team-size data

A survey of UX teams found that a discovery had about four people working on it full time on average, most often a designer, a researcher, and a product or project manager. This is the empirical anchor for the article's "core team of two to four people" guidance.

### Role coverage

For software projects specifically, NN/g's findings are consistent with a delivery-side team that includes a business analyst, a designer, a solution architect or tech lead, and a project manager — often with one person covering two of the roles.
