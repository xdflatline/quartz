## Overview

Oleksandr Tytarenko is the author of the source article on the discovery phase in software development, published on dev.to and on the Discovery Phase AI blog. He is also the builder of Discovery Phase AI, a vendor tool that targets the writing-and-assembling side of a software-project discovery. The article discloses the relationship.

## Key Details

### Article

The article is a practical guide to running a discovery phase: the steps in order, who takes part, the ten deliverables, week-by-week plans for 2-, 4-, and 6-week discoveries, how long discovery takes, what it costs, how it fits into agile and waterfall projects, red flags in a discovery proposal, common mistakes, when to skip discovery, and a FAQ. The article is drafted with AI assistance and edited by a human before publishing, per the author's own disclosure.

### Product

Discovery Phase AI drafts the early stages (background, problem statement, stakeholders, goals, users and journeys, requirements) from the decks, documents, call transcripts, and images a team already has, and exports a single report. The team still runs the interviews, reviews every draft, and makes the decisions.
