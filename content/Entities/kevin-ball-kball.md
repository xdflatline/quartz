# Kevin Ball (KBall)

**Source:** [[Raw/sedaily-episode-1951-pinecone-nexus-2026]]
**Category:** Person / Podcast Host / Engineering Leader
**Current role (2026):** VP of Engineering at Mento
**Podcast:** [Software Engineering Daily](https://softwareengineeringdaily.com)
**Website:** kball.llc

## Overview

Host of Software Engineering Daily, the most-cited podcast in this wiki for engineering-deep dives on infrastructure, AI, and systems topics. KBall's interview style — long-form, technically detailed, with the guest doing most of the explaining — is why the SED transcripts (including [[Raw/sedaily-episode-1951-pinecone-nexus-2026]]) are usable as **primary sources** for extracting architectural concepts.

## Background

- **Current:** VP of Engineering at **Mento**; independent coach for engineers and engineering leaders.
- **Past:** Co-founder and CTO of two companies.
- **Community:** Founded the San Diego JavaScript Meetup; organises the AI in Action discussion group through Latent Space.
- **Online:** Active on Twitter and LinkedIn (links via show notes).

## Why he's relevant to this wiki

- **Primary source:** Software Engineering Daily transcripts are first-class sources for [[Concepts/]] pages — the long-form format gives guests room to articulate design rationale, trade-offs, and outlook in a way that blog posts rarely do.
- **Curator role in concept extraction:** Several concepts already in this wiki (and [[Research/]]) derive from SED episodes. KBall is the consistent interviewer across them.
