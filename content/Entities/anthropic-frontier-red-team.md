## Overview

The Anthropic Frontier Red Team is the unit within [[Entities/anthropic|Anthropic]] that studies frontier model behavior in adversarial and multi-agent settings. It publishes the **Frontier Red Team newsletter** ([subscribe](https://www.anthropic.com/research/multiagent-systems)) and produces research posts on red-teaming findings.

## Notable publications

- **Patterns and Problems in Emerging Multiagent Systems** (Aug 13, 2026) — a study of how frontier Claude models behave in multi-agent settings, covering coordination gains, conformity failures, collusion, epistemic deficits, and incompatible-goal escalation. Source: [[Raw/anthropic-multiagent-systems-2026-08-13|Raw]].
