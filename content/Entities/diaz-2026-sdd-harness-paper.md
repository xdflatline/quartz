## What it is

A conceptual analysis paper drawing on vision papers, practitioner reports, talks, and tooling to characterize:

1. A socio-technical model of Spec-Driven Development (SDD) in which specifications act as the contract substrate between humans and agents.
2. An operational characterization of the harness — the technical harness around the agent and the methodological harness around the team — with eight worked mechanisms (knowledge management, production support, governance).
3. A typology of five recurring human–agent interaction patterns under SDD.
4. A research agenda organized into three levels (human competences, team dynamics, organizational adoption).

## Why it is presented as "a first step, not a theory"

The authors are explicit about the evidence base: peer-reviewed ASE studies and a shared academic–industrial vocabulary do not yet exist. The expected benefits (consistency, absorption, transferability, compounding) are framed as testable predictions, not findings. The paper is positioned as a step toward academic–industrial consensus.
