# Charity Majors

**Source:** [Honeycomb.io](https://www.honeycomb.io/) — Newman: "Charity pays me in stickers. Just to be really clear. I always think I work independently. I have to be very clear."
**Category:** Engineer / CEO / Observability Practitioner

## Overview

Charity Majors is the CEO of Honeycomb and one of the most vocal practitioners of the "production is truth" stance in the industry. Newman repeatedly credits her framing when discussing observability, SLOs, SLIs, and the limits of static documentation. Her position — adopted by Newman — is that structured events flowing out of production are the only place the system's actual behavior lives.

## Newman's attribution in the interview

- *"See, I feel like I've got to channel my friend Charity at this point and say the truth is production. Everything else is the lies we tell ourselves."*
- On the OTEL/Honeycomb stack: *"Luckily we've got things like OTEL now... although Honeycomb are the best. Charity pays me in stickers."*
- On SLOs/SLIs: Newman's framing matches the Honeycomb school; "vanity dashboards" vs. SLOs is directly from this lineage.

## Concepts she's associated with

- **Observability as a property of a system** (not a tool stack) — the more observable, the easier to understand from external signals.
- **Production is truth** — the source of the wiki's [[Concepts/production-is-truth]] page.
- **Blameless post-mortems** and incident review culture — overlapping with [[Concepts/post-incident-review-culture]].
- **Cognitive surrender as a concern** — discussed in Honeycomb-adjacent writing on AI in incident response.
