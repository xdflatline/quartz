# Practical Data Modeling (Substack)

**Category:** Article (publication)
**Website:** https://practicaldatamodeling.substack.com/

---

## Overview

*Practical Data Modeling* is Joe Reis's Substack newsletter, launched in 2024, that publishes chapters-in-progress of a forthcoming data-modeling book alongside essays on industry practice. It reached >20,000 subscribers by August 2026 and is the canonical publication venue for the Mixed Model Arts (MMA) framing. The newsletter is reader-supported (free and paid tiers).

## Key Details

- **Publisher:** Joe Reis
- **Format:** Long-form essays, chapter drafts, commentary
- **Subscribers:** >20,000 (Aug 2026)
- **Tagline:** "Whether you're a beginner or an experienced data professional interested in leveling up your data modeling, we will help you take your skills to the next level."
