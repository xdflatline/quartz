## Overview

Antigravity CLI is Google's agent-first terminal tool and unified platform, which launched in May 2026. In May 2026, version 2.0 of the platform topped the OpenSCAD architectural 3D LLM benchmark, demonstrating high precision in generating 3D models.

## Architectural Achievements

- **OpenSCAD LLM Benchmark**: Antigravity 2.0 outperformed all other frameworks in generating structured 3D spatial models from architectural descriptions.
