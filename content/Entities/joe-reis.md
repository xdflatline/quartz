# Joe Reis

**Category:** Person

---

## Overview

Joe Reis is a data engineer, author, and educator best known as co-author (with Matt Housley) of *Fundamentals of Data Engineering* (O'Reilly, 2022), a widely used practitioner's textbook that re-frames data engineering around data lifecycle stages rather than tooling camps. He publishes the Substack newsletter *Practical Data Modeling* (>20,000 subscribers as of Aug 2026) and is the originator of the **Mixed Model Arts (MMA)** framing: a call to unify OLTP, OLAP, semantic, and agent-facing data modeling around first principles rather than methodology tribalisms.

## Key Details

- **Books:** *Fundamentals of Data Engineering* (O'Reilly, 2022, with Matt Housley). Available in revised/updated editions.
- **Newsletter:** *Practical Data Modeling* (Substack). Mixes chapters-in-progress from a forthcoming book on data modeling with commentary on industry practice. Posts published 2024–2026 include "What is (and isn't) Data Modeling?" (Apr 2024), "Semantics, Ontology, Taxonomy, and Metadata" (Nov 2025), "Ch 1 - The Era of the Mixed Model Artist" (Feb 2026), and the *Mixed Model Arts Manifesto* (Aug 19, 2026).
- **Position:** Argues for data modeling as a unified, continuous, first-principles discipline; rejects Kimball/Inmon, normalized/denormalized, and application/analytical silos as outdated; insists models must be designed for both human cognition and AI-agent reasoning in the Agentic Era.
- **Methodology metaphor:** Mixed Model Arts explicitly borrows from mixed martial arts ("train everything, apply what works"), as a frame for ending "my style beats your style" tribalism.
