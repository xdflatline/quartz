## Overview

NVIDIA is a semiconductor and AI-accelerator company. It enters this wiki through two paths.

**As an employer.** [[Entities/sid-pardeshi]] joined NVIDIA in 2016, before the company's market re-rating, and worked there through the AI acceleration wave. He describes the company as having operated much like a startup during that period — an unusual characterisation, and the origin of his thesis that AI applied to software development at enterprise scale was a large enough opportunity to found a company around.

**As an archetype of enterprise-scale engineering.** The episode's discussion of codebase scale — tens of millions of lines for a single product, multiple codebases of that size, hundreds to thousands of active committers, patterns buried deep enough that a 20-line change can violate a compliance or load-balancing constraint — describes exactly the environment [[Concepts/codebase-knowledge-graph-context-substrate]] is built for. The constraint is not that no one knows the patterns; it is that no single agent can hold them at the moment of change.
