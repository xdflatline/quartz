# Terminus-2

**Role:** Strong human-designed coding-agent harness
**Used in:** [[Entities/meta-harness-paper]] experiments on TerminalBench-2

## Overview

A strong human-designed coding-agent harness, used as a **starting point** for Meta-Harness experiments on TerminalBench-2. The fact that Meta-Harness can improve on Terminus-2 from a fixed model (Claude 3.5 Sonnet) is the evidence that the harness design space has room above strong human design.

## Why It Matters

The Meta-Harness paper is one of the cleanest results in the harness-evolution literature. The starting points are deliberately strong (Terminus-2, Terminus-KIRA) to avoid the trivial "ML beats naive baseline" result. The improvement from this strong starting point is what makes the Meta-Harness claim credible.
