# James Lewis

**Source:** [martinfowler.com/microservices](https://martinfowler.com/articles/microservices.html)
**Category:** Software Architect

## Overview

James Lewis is a software architect at ThoughtWorks who, at an internal architectural symposium in the English Lake District (~2011–12), pitched a pattern he had been observing across the firms he was consulting for. Originally calling it "micro apps", he was corrected by someone in the (mostly hungover) room of ~10 attendees to "services". The name stuck.

Lewis and Martin Fowler co-wrote the first article on the pattern, published on Fowler's website. Newman's 2015 *Building Microservices* book extended the framing into the practitioner manual that popularized the term across the industry.

## Why this matters

Before the name, Netflix referred to their architecture as "fine-grained SOA"; the microservices label catalyzed the "semantic diffusion" Newman describes — every SOA project retroactively relabeled itself microservices. The 2011–2012 timing was the fuse; the post-2015 bushy growth was the fire.
