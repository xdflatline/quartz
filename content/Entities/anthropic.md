## Overview

Anthropic is an AI safety research company and developer of Claude, a family of large language models. In May 2026, the company saw significant developments including high-profile talent acquisition and strategic acquisitions of its own.

## Key Events (2026)

### Karpathy Joins Anthropic (2026-05)
- Andrej Karpathy (former Tesla AI director, OpenAI researcher) joined Anthropic
- Announced via Twitter/X, received 1253+ points on Hacker News
- Strengthens Anthropic's research team significantly
- Signals Anthropic's growing influence in the AI research community[[hn-ive-joined-anthropic-2026-05-20|Source: hn-ive-joined-anthropic-2026-05-20]]

### Stainless Acquisition (2026-05)
- Anthropic acquired Stainless, an SDK generation company
- Part of vertical integration strategy: model provider → developer tools → agent platform
- See [[ai-agents|ai-agents]] for details on the broader agent ecosystem

### Frontier Red Team: Multi-Agent Failure-Mode Study (2026-08)
- The [[anthropic-frontier-red-team|Anthropic Frontier Red Team]] published [Patterns and Problems in Emerging Multiagent Systems](https://www.anthropic.com/research/multiagent-systems) (Aug 13, 2026), a systematic study of how frontier Claude models behave in multi-agent settings.
- Documents four failure-mode families: [[agent-low-variance-conformity|conformity collapse]], [[agent-collusion-pattern|collusion]], [[agent-epistemic-vigilance-deficit|epistemic blindness]], and [[agent-turf-war-escalation|incompatible-goal escalation]].
- See [[Raw/anthropic-multiagent-systems-2026-08-13|Raw]] and the [[Research/anthropic-multiagent-failure-modes-2026-08|research index]] for synthesis.

## Position in AI Landscape

- One of the major model providers alongside OpenAI, Google, and Mistral
- Known for focus on AI safety and constitutional AI approach
- Claude competes with GPT-4/5, Gemini, and open-weight models
