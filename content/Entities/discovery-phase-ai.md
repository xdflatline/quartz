# Discovery Phase AI

## Overview

Discovery Phase AI is a vendor tool built by the article's author, Oleksandr Tytarenko. The product targets the writing-and-assembling side of a software-project discovery: it drafts the early stages (background, problem statement, stakeholders, goals, users and journeys, requirements) from the documents, decks, call transcripts, and images a team already has, helps write and score the rest stage by stage, and exports a single report. The user reviews every draft before anything is saved; the team still runs the interviews, makes the decisions, and owns the risk log.

## Key Details

### What it does

- **Drafts the first stages from existing material.** Given decks, prior specs, transcripts, and screenshots, the tool produces first drafts of the background, problem statement, stakeholder map, goals, user journeys, and requirements.
- **Helps write and score the later stages stage by stage.** The tool structures the requirements-list scoring and the architecture decision records, with human review at each step.
- **Exports a single discovery report.** The output is the same ten-deliverable set described in the article.

### What it does not do

The article is explicit: AI is good at the writing and the checking, not at the decisions. Discovery Phase AI does not interview users, does not judge which stakeholder is right, and does not own a risk. The team still does those things.

### Disclosure

The article discloses the author's relationship with the product. The article's framing of "AI helps with discovery, but the human decisions stay human" is consistent with the product's described behavior and with the article's own advice that the deliverables must remain owned by the client.
