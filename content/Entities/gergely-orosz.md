## Overview

Gergely Orosz writes *The Pragmatic Engineer Newsletter* and hosts *The Pragmatic Engineer Podcast*, focused on software engineering at Big Tech and startups, "from the inside". His interview format pairs experienced practitioners (engineers, tech leads, CTOs) for ~2-hour conversations covering their career paths and hard-earned lessons.

## Background referenced in the source

- Was at Uber from 2016 to 2018 (during the period the *Building Microservices* book was reaching mainstream adoption). Worked in distributed-systems-heavy contexts.
- Has had follow-up conversations with Uber CTO Twan Palm about the microservices-at-Uber story.
- Recurring guest network includes Sam Newman, Charity Majors, Addy Osmani, and many others.
