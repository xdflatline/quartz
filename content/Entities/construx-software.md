## Overview

Construx Software is a software-engineering consultancy founded by Steve McConnell. The firm publishes reference material on software estimation and project management, including the canonical 2019 white paper on the cone of uncertainty — the model that describes how software-estimate error narrows as decisions are made, and which is the empirical foundation for the practice of upfront discovery phases and ranged pre-build estimates.

## Key Details

### Cone of uncertainty white paper

The Construx white paper presents the cone of uncertainty as a best-case model: at the initial-concept stage, a software estimate can plausibly be off by a factor of four in either direction, and the range narrows only as concrete requirements and design decisions are made. Time alone does not narrow the cone; decisions do. The paper is the canonical citation in any argument for a discovery phase as a stage whose purpose is to make the narrowing decisions on purpose.

### Other resources

Construx publishes books, white papers, and training material on software estimation, project management, and code construction. The firm is closely associated with McConnell's larger body of work (*Code Complete*, *Rapid Development*, *Software Estimation: Demystifying the Black Art*).
