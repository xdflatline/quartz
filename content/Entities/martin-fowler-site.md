# Martin Fowler's Site

## Overview

[martinfowler.com](https://martinfowler.com) is the long-running personal site of Martin Fowler, a Chief Scientist at [[Entities/thoughtworks]]. It is widely cited as a cornerstone of modern software engineering writing — covering refactoring, agile methods, software architecture, design patterns, and continuous delivery. The site also publishes guest articles by Thoughtworkers and invited contributors; *The Orchestrator's Tax* by [[Entities/rahul-garg]] (16 July 2026) is one such guest piece.

## Key Details

- **Editorial pattern:** The site is curated by Fowler personally, with feedback and guidance acknowledged in the bylines of guest posts. [[Entities/rahul-garg]] thanks Fowler for guidance and feedback on the orchestrator's-tax article.
- **Distinguished from Fowler's employer:** martinfowler.com is a personal publication, not a Thoughtworks corporate property. The Thoughtworks [[Entities/thoughtworks]] Insights blog is a separate publication; thoughts and opinions on martinfowler.com are the author's, not necessarily their employer's position.
- **Relevance to the wiki:** Several research threads cite Fowler's work directly or via guest authors; the orchestrator's-tax post is the most recent addition to the wiki.
