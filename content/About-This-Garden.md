# About This Garden

## Purpose

This digital garden is a public knowledge base where I cultivate ideas, document learnings, and share Resources. It's built with **Quartz** and written in **Obsidian-flavored Markdown**.

## Philosophy

> **Grow in public. Connect ideas. Embrace imperfection.**

- Notes are living documents — they evolve over time
- Wikilinks (`[[Note Name]]`) create a web of knowledge
- The graph view reveals unexpected connections
- Publishing early invites feedback and serendipity
